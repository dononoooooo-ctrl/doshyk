Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Doshyk is a private content management and publishing tool used to manage and publish short-form video content to TikTok.

Information We Process

When a user authorizes Doshyk through TikTok, the application may process information made available through the permissions granted by the user, including basic account information and information required to perform authorized content publishing actions.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the application's content management and TikTok publishing functionality.

Authorization

Doshyk accesses TikTok features only after explicit user authorization. Users may revoke access through TikTok where supported.

Data Sharing

Doshyk does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except as necessary to operate the authorized functionality or comply with applicable requirements.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to operate the application and its authorized workflows.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the application owner through the official project repository.
